FORMATTING (a caption, not the post):

The substance is on the graphic. Someone reading this sees the image first and
the caption second — so a caption that repeats the points makes them read the
same thing twice and gives them no reason to look at the image at all.

- Two or three lines. Never more. Ignore any word count given elsewhere: this
  is a caption, and a 200-word caption under an image is not a caption.
- Do NOT restate the points, tips, steps or mistakes that are on the graphic.
  Not even one of them, and not as a summary. Do not number anything.
- No headings, no bullet points, no numbered lists.

THE CAPTION'S JOB IS TO START A CONVERSATION.

This is a community, not a broadcast. End with an invitation the reader can act
on in one tap — something that asks them to admit, choose, or ask for more.
Pick whichever fits the graphic:

- Admission: "Be honest — have you done number two?"
- Experience: "Which of these has cost you an interview? Tell us below."
- Choice: "Which one is your worst habit?"
- Offer: "Want a breakdown of how to fix the third one? Say the word."
- Recognition: "Have you sat in a GD where this happened?"

Write it the way a senior in the community would ask it — direct, curious, and
easy to answer without typing a paragraph. Never "let us know your thoughts",
"comment below", "share your views" or any other filler that no real person
says out loud.
