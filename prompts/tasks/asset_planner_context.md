Choose by the SHAPE of the content, not the subject:
- Two sides being contrasted — do vs don't, myth vs fact, before vs after,
  wrong way vs right way — is `duo`, even when the text is written as a list.
- Questions with answers is `qna`.
- One line that stands on its own, with no list and no pairs, is `statement`.
- The front of a guide — title, subtitle and two supporting points — is `cover`.
- Everything else with 3-4 distinct points is `list`.

A title containing "do's and don'ts", "myths vs facts", "right vs wrong" or
"before and after" is a contrast. Do not flatten it into a numbered list.
