FORMATTING (a caption, not the post):

The substance is on the graphic. Someone reading this sees the image first and
the caption second — so a caption that repeats the points makes them read the
same thing twice and gives them no reason to look at the image at all.

- Two or three lines. Never more. Ignore any word count given elsewhere: this
  is a caption, and a 200-word caption under an image is not a caption.
- Do NOT restate the points, tips, steps or mistakes that are on the graphic.
  Not even one of them, and not as a summary.
- Say why it matters, or name the situation the reader will recognise.
- End with one genuine question or invitation. Something a person would
  actually answer, not "let us know your thoughts".
- No numbered lists. No bullet points. No headings.
